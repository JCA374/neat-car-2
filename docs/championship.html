The Championship

Each car is a training recipe built from what the ladders taught us, trained once from scratch on the same laptop, for at most 8 hours. Then they race on five tracks that none of them has ever seen.

⏱ Quickest to finish

The checkpoint that first finished all five validation tracks during training. On each race track, the car that finishes with the least training time wins.

🏁 Fastest lap

The checkpoint with the fastest validation laps within the 8-hour budget. On each race track, the fastest lap wins.

Points per track: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8. A DNF scores nothing. Both checkpoints are chosen on separate validation tracks, so the race tracks stay unseen.
