NEAT · neuroevolution of augmenting topologies

NEAT: networks that evolve their own shape

A population of 100 small networks starts with the 40 inputs wired straight to the 3 controls. In each generation, every network drives the same few random tracks. The best breed, and their children get mutated weights, plus the occasional new connection or hidden node. There's no gradient, just selection. Because each generation's networks drive independently, all CPU cores stay busy.

Each card is one step. Its model is the generation that scored best on 5 held-out validation tracks, and it's benchmarked on 5 more tracks that no step ever trains on.
