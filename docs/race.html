NEAT vs PPO, head to head

The current champion of each model is lined up, and you can add any other step. CarRacing simulates one car at a time, so this is a ghost race: each driver's real benchmark drive on the track is replayed side by side, in sync. The cars pass through each other, and the first to finish the lap wins. A driver gets up to 40 seconds.

    Championship: all five tracks

    The selected drivers race on every track and score F1-style points per finishing position (25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4). A finished lap beats any unfinished run, and a faster lap beats a slower one. Unfinished runs are ranked by how much of the track they covered.

    A lap only counts when the car has touched every road tile. A driver that cuts the last corner across the grass can pass right beside the finish line and still not finish: it shows as DNF · missed 1 tile.

    Data: the benchmark drives recorded by scripts/run_step.py. These are the same drives behind every lap time on the site.
