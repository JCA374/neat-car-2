Ghost race · same track, same 20 seconds

Race the drivers

Pick a track and a few drivers from the project's history, NEAT and PPO, and press Start. CarRacing simulates one car at a time, so this is a ghost race: each driver's real drive on that track is replayed side by side in sync. The cars pass through each other, and nobody can block anybody. These are the exact drives behind the benchmark scores. A race lasts 1000 frames (20 seconds), and finishing a lap ends a driver's run.
